About

Presently, I am serving as a lecturer at Atish Dipankar University of Science and Technology (Full-time). I teach and supervise courses in apparel and textile engineering, and focus on sustainability and machine learning based projects. Apart from teaching, I build lightweight demos with Google Colab, MATLAB for limited local storage and easy sharing.

Education

I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Textile Engineering (Apparel) at the Bangladesh University of Textiles (BUTEX), where my thesis research is ongoing. Prior to that, I completed my B.Sc. in Textile Engineering (Apparel) at BUTEX (43th Batch) with a CGPA of 3.84 out of 4.00.
